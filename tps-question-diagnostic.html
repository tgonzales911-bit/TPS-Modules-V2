Sample Diagnosis · Live Example
"You have a 22-year veteran who has stopped showing initiative and seems disengaged from the crew. How do you handle it?"
Bucket
Personnel Issue — this is a performance/engagement question, not a conflict question. The keyword 'disengaged' and 'veteran' point directly to the 3 U's diagnostic framework.
L · Label
Open by naming the framework: "Before I respond, I need to diagnose whether this is an Unaware, Unable, or Unwilling situation — each requires a completely different approach."
E · Engage
Apply the 3 U's explicitly to this scenario — a 22-year vet is almost certainly not Unaware or Unable. The question is whether this is Unwilling (disengaged by choice) or something else entirely.
A · Apply
At Lieutenant: private direct conversation, Skill/Will Matrix diagnosis, Bank of Leadership investment before any corrective action.
D · Deliver
Close by naming what this answer means at your target rank: "As a Lieutenant, handling this well means my crew sees that I hold the standard — and that I do it with enough relational equity that it lands as leadership, not discipline."
Prescription
Frameworks: 3 U's (diagnose before responding), Bank of Leadership (deposits before withdrawals with a 22-year vet), T·C·H·O·E·D·S (Humility is your entry point).
What Assessors Are Scoring
Whether you diagnose before you respond. Assessors are watching for the 3 U's instinct — do you identify WHY the problem exists before selecting your intervention? Candidates who jump straight to discipline score 3/5. Candidates who diagnose first score 4–5/5.
Response Architecture — Follow This Sequence
1
Open by naming the supervisory principle: 'Before I decide how to respond, I need to understand whether this is an Unaware, Unable, or Unwilling situation — because each requires a different approach.'
2
Gather information first — meet with the individual privately, review documentation, establish the facts.
3
Apply the 3 U's diagnosis explicitly — name which U you believe applies and why.
4
Select your intervention based on the diagnosis — development for Unaware/Unable, progressive documentation for Unwilling.
5
Close with a specific follow-up plan and documentation commitment.
